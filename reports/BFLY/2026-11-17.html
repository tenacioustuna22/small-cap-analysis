Scenarios, 3 and 5 years out

Base case, 3 years: $1.22 a share (-79%), within a range of $0.310 to $3.15.

Read before the figures.
  • Nothing reacts. No case assumes cost cuts, a sale or a restructuring. In the bear a real company would act long before its cash ran out, and raising about $100M when the business is worth about $85M is unlikely. Read the bear as what happens if nothing changes, not as a forecast.
  • New shares are sold only when cash hits exactly zero. Real companies raise with about a year of cash left, often at worse prices, so the new-share counts are understated, most of all in the bear.
  • The base case never breaks even: its revenue barely grows (about $132M in 2027, $136M in 2031). Management's "cash flow positive in 2028" needs revenue near its own plan.
  • Values are not discounted to today, count no cash earned after breakeven, and leave out 19.3M potential shares from options and warrants.
  • Today's price is above even the bull case. The market is pricing something these cases leave out, such as management's plan or a wider platform view of Embedded. See research/BFLY.md, "What the market is pricing".
  • Not in any path: Midjourney stopping payment (possible only through breach or insolvency), the SPAC-era class action ($14.3M accrued, $13.7M expected from insurance) if insurance falls short, Midjourney's license upgrade option, and any renewal after 2030.
