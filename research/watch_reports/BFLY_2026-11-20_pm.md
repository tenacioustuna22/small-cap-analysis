- [W5] Exo Imaging received an $11.6M grant from the Gates Foundation, announced 2026-11-19. The
  foundation's record gives the committed amount as $11,594,471, dated September 2026, over 28
  months. The purpose is "to develop a low-cost, AI-enabled, multi-use point-of-care ultrasound
  platform" for non-expert health workers in low- and middle-income countries. The release
  describes Exo's "piezoelectric silicon hardware" with AI that runs on the device. It says the

  Exo's second Gates grant: the first was $1,000,000 in April 2025, over 12 months.
