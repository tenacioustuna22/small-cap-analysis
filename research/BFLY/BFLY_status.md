## In short

**The thesis is unchanged since 2026-11-18.** There is no new Embedded deal, and nothing significant
has happened at the partners. Money keeps flowing to BFLY's kind of buyer: brain-interface funding
rounds, and AI spending by big tech.

**Worth watching:** Exo Imaging, the main rival chip maker, said in a 2026-11-19 release that it
is building for "OEM-integrated systems". That is a first hint that it may sell its chip to other
device makers. No customer has been named.

**Today, 2026-11-23:** the court hears Midjourney's motion for judgment on the pleadings in the
studios' lawsuit. The judge may rule from the bench or later; a ruling is sent the same day.

## Recent changes, newest first

- **2026-11-20/21, W3:** In the studios' lawsuit, a joint mediation report (contents not public on
  CourtListener), then a studio motion to compel Midjourney's compliance with "its prior agreement
  and the Court's orders" regarding the v8 and "torch-aesthetic-redux" source code. Neutral for
  now: no ruling, settlement or damages figure. The hearing on Midjourney's motion is today.
- **2026-11-17, W1:** Butterfly will present at the Evercore Healthcare Conference on 2026-12-01.
  Neutral: a conference notice, with no deal or financial news.
- **2026-11-19, W5:** Exo's Gates grant and the "OEM-integrated systems" wording. Neutral, leaning
  weaker: it competes with Core in global health, and no chip customer has been named yet.
- **2026-11-19, W2:** Sonic Incytes and Madrigal sign a deal for liver assessment. Neutral, or
  slightly positive if the device turns out to use Butterfly's chip.
- **2026-11-17/18, W5:** Science Corp's $400M round at a reported $3.5B\*. Supportive: big
  brain-interface rounds are still getting done (its implant uses light, not ultrasound).
- **2026-11-17, W6:** Nvidia's data-center revenue up 110%, with Q4 revenue guided to $130.0B ±3%.
  Supportive: the AI build-out is still growing.
- **2026-11-12, W5:** Synchron pauses trial enrollment. Neutral for BFLY.
